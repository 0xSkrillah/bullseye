# Frontend demo runbook · 90 seconds

Purpose: make the product understandable before explaining its machinery. The full submission video may be longer; confirm official duration. This is the customer-facing portion, not proof of a new trading strategy.

**0–15s:** Open Market Desk at a selected, permitted real or explicitly recorded event. “Bullseye explains what changed in a tokenised asset and checks the evidence.” Show asset, event time and first-detected time.

**15–35s:** Point to one actual before/after fact and the financial relevance. “This is a balance adjustment, not a forecast that the stock will rise.” Show no-executable-quotes disclosure where applicable; unknown inputs stay unknown.

**35–50s:** Use the exact-Brief action. Show what the report includes, its price and payment-network/testnet label. Open one permitted evidence source. No terminal or internal ID is needed to understand the offer.

**50–75s:** Demonstrate the existing buyer flow in an approved environment. If showing offline fixture purchase, say so. If only a recorded prior purchase is available, switch to a clearly labelled recording. If payment is unknown, show recovery, not a second purchase or invented success.

**75–90s:** Open the received report/JSON and optional receipt. End: “One checked intelligence report, usable by a human or another agent.” Then optionally show Situation Room as evidence of the automated process, not as the product introduction.

## Before recording
Fixed viewport1280x720 or1440x900. No unrelated notifications. Preselect appropriate event; no hardcoded fake records or state transitions. Keep testnet and provenance labels visible. Reduce motion. Ensure exact route and recovery work after reload. Sanitize any owner capability; never record a seed phrase or raw signing payload.

## Fallback
A stored screenshot/recording is dated historical evidence, not “live”. Keep a local offline flow with recorded data and labelled fixture model/payment. Do not claim the current deployed branch had a purchase if it has not.
