# Claim ledger

Every claim Bullseye makes about itself, what backs it, and what does not. If a claim is not in
the "Verified" table with evidence you can run or open, do not repeat it in a pitch, a README or
a demo. Last reviewed: 18 September 2026.

Status values: **VERIFIED** (evidence in this repo, reproducible) · **PARTIAL** (mechanism built
and tested, but not against the real external system) · **BLOCKED** (cannot be done yet; reason
given) · **NOT CLAIMED**.

Rows V18 to V21 were P1, P2, P3 and B2 until the first live run on 18 September 2026. Each is
scoped to what was observed that day: one investigation and one testnet settlement.

## Not claimed

Guaranteed alpha or profit · investment advice · autonomous trading · customer revenue · that
testnet or fixture payments are revenue · that estimated contribution is profit · evidence of
demand · mainnet settlement · Brief quality, a gate rejection rate, a settlement success rate, a
typical cost per Brief or a time per investigation (one live investigation and one live
settlement exist; neither is a sample) · detector recall (nothing measures events it misses) ·
real-time detection (no latency has been measured; the detector polls) · comprehensive market
coverage (one signal type, one issuer, one chain) · that a content hash proves a source was
truthful (it proves the Brief and the stored responses have not changed) · marketplace approval.

## The strongest honest statement today

"On 18 September 2026 Bullseye detected a QSRx dividend rebase from the issuer's public record,
confirmed it independently against X Layer contract state, and took it once through the whole
path: a budgeted live-model investigation that cost $0.071725 as billed against a $0.60 ceiling,
a deterministic publication gate that rejected the first draft and published the revision, and
an x402 purchase that settled through the OKX facilitator on X Layer testnet and was verified
on-chain in block 41310183. That is one investigation and one testnet settlement. The payment
was made with test tokens and is not revenue, and nothing here measures quality, reliability or
demand."
